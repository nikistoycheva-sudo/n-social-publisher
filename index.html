N Social Publisher

Social media publishing and analytics

A private desktop tool used only by the owners of the N Nails and Na Nokti brands.

What it does

  • Publishes our own photos and videos to our own Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok accounts.
  • Reads statistics for those accounts (views, followers, engagement) and shows them in a private dashboard.
  • Reads and replies to comments on our own posts.

The tool connects only through the official APIs of Meta, Google (YouTube) and TikTok, after the account owner signs in and approves access. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts of its own.

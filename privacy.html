Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

N Social Publisher ("the tool") is a private desktop application used by the owners of the N Nails and Na Nokti brands to manage their own social media accounts. This policy explains what data the tool accesses and how it is handled.

1. Data we access

Only after the account owner signs in and approves access, the tool may access, through the official platform APIs:

  • Profile information: account name, ID, profile picture and basic profile details.
  • Content: the account's own posts and videos, including titles, captions and publishing status.
  • Analytics: statistics such as views, reach, watch time, followers, likes, shares and comment counts, and for YouTube, estimated revenue where available.
  • Comments and messages: comments on the account's own posts and, where enabled, messages sent to the account, so that the owner can read and reply to them.

2. How we use it

The data is used only to publish the owner's content, to show the owner statistics about their own accounts, and to let the owner respond to comments. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Access tokens and downloaded statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer, in files readable only by the owner's user account. We do not run a server that stores this data, and we do not sell, rent or share it with anyone.

4. Google and YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Retention

Data is kept only as long as the owner needs it for the purposes above and is deleted from the owner's computer when access is revoked or the tool is no longer used.

6. Revoking access

You can revoke the tool's access at any time:

  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Facebook / Instagram: Facebook Settings → Business integrations
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions

7. Children

The tool is not intended for, and does not knowingly process data from, children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
